# Visual Rhythm & Motion Density Rules

1. **One Primary Motion Event per Beat:** The viewer's attention must never be divided between competing high-intensity animations. One element leads; supporting elements remain subordinate.
2. **Motion Density Cap:** A single beat may have at most 1 primary (intensity $\ge 3$) and 2 supporting (intensity $\le 2$) layers.
3. **Intentional Stillness:** Still frames and low-intensity holds (Level 0 and Level 1) are essential contrast. Do not force high kinetic movement across every second of video.
4. **Anti-Freeze Camera Drift:** When an asset or card holds on screen for $>3.0\text{s}$, apply sub-pixel harmonic drift (`camera.push_in` at $0.035\times$) to keep the canvas alive without distracting from text.
5. **Pacing Continuity:** Avoid changing visual styles or formats rapidly within the same section. Maintain format consistency across related beats.

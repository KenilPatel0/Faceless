# Sound Effect Library & Taxonomy

## Sound Design Philosophy
Sound effects are punctuation, not wallpaper. Silence is an intentional design choice. Every sound cue must serve one of six functions:
1. **Explain**: UI click confirming an action, rising tone tracking processing.
2. **Emphasize**: Subtle bass hit or chime marking a key breakthrough.
3. **Connect**: Short whoosh accompanying an architectural camera push or section change.
4. **Locate**: Subtle server hum or quiet studio air establishing context.
5. **Characterize**: Physical click of a terminal switch.
6. **Punctuate**: Clean chime at a recap or conversion completion.

## SFX Taxonomy
- **UI Micro-interactions**: `soft_click`, `tap`, `toggle`, `keyboard_tap`
- **Digital / Technology**: `data_pulse`, `scan`, `digital_blip`, `vector_chime`
- **Transitions**: `short_whoosh`, `soft_sweep`, `airy_pass`
- **Impacts / Reveals**: `low_thud`, `shimmer_reveal`, `milestone_chime`
- **Alert / Feedback**: `success_chime`, `warning_tick`

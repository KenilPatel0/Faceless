# thumbnail-prompt — Authentic, High-CTR ChatGPT Thumbnail Prompt Engine

This skill transforms any video concept, script, or topic into high-converting thumbnail prompts specifically designed for **ChatGPT (DALL-E 3)** and **Midjourney**.

The core objective is to eliminate the cheesy, plastic, oversaturated "AI look" and replace it with authentic, tangible editorial photography that triggers irresistible viewer curiosity.

---

## 1. Why Most AI Thumbnails Fail (The "AI Look" Traps)

Viewers sub-consciously recognize and skip generic AI-generated thumbnails because of these telltale signs:
1. **The Sci-Fi Neon Trap:** Random floating cyan/magenta holograms, glowing neural grids, and floating abstract shapes with no physical weight.
2. **The Plastic Sheen:** Hyper-smooth, waxy textures with zero real-world imperfections (dust, micro-scratches, matte surfaces).
3. **Overcrowded Chaos:** Too many objects competing for attention, making the image indecipherable at 1.5-inch mobile size.

---

## 2. The Formula for Authentic, "Non-AI" Prompts

To force ChatGPT / DALL-E 3 to produce authentic, curiosity-inducing photography, every prompt must specify **5 mandatory layers**:

| Layer | Purpose | Exact Keywords to Use |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Camera & Optics** | Forces real physical lens distortion and depth of field | `Shot on 35mm lens, f/1.8 aperture, shallow depth of field, macro photography, natural bokeh` |
| **2. Lighting** | Eliminates artificial neon glow; grounds the scene in reality | `Directional tungsten desk lamp, soft window daylight, authentic chiaroscuro, natural shadows` |
| **3. Material Realism** | Forces realistic physical imperfections | `Matte silicon surface, visible copper solder traces, heatpipe textures, subtle dust motes, Kodak Portra 400 film grain` |
| **4. Negative Space** | Leaves a clean area for on-screen text | `Clean, uncluttered composition with generous negative space on the left third` |
| **5. Negative Directives** | Explicitly instructs ChatGPT what to suppress | `No cheesy neon holograms, no fantasy glowing blue particles, no glossy 3D plastic render, no cartoonish elements` |

## 3. The 3 Curiosity Archetypes for Tech Videos

### Archetype 1: The Tangible Physical Contrast (Best for Tech/Speed/Math)
Places an impossibly fast digital concept into a tangible real-world physical object.
- **Example Concept:** *How AI Answers in Milliseconds*
- **Prompt:**
  > *"A documentary-style macro photograph of a real industrial server motherboard resting on an engineer's wooden workbench. In sharp focus is an authentic matte-black silicon AI accelerator processor with exposed copper heatpipes and micro-printed circuit numbers. Resting right beside the chip is an authentic vintage analog stopwatch, its mechanical red needle frozen at exactly 0.015 seconds. Natural soft daylight from a window, shallow depth of field, subtle film grain. Clean empty space on the left side. No futuristic holograms, no glowing neon trails, no 3D render look, no text."*

### Archetype 2: The Engineer's Desk / Real Workspace (Best for Coding/AI Tools)
Shows real human context: notes, coffee, hardware, and physical work rather than generic digital cyberspace.
- **Example Concept:** *Is Coding Dead? / AI Agents*
- **Prompt:**
  > *"An authentic editorial photograph taken over the shoulder of a software developer's minimalist desk at night. On the wooden desk sits a warm ceramic coffee mug and a printed sheet of code marked up with an aggressive red pen circling three critical lines. The computer screen in the soft background is out of focus. Warm tungsten lamp light casting natural soft shadows, shot on 50mm f/2 lens, natural documentary realism. No robotic cyborg hands, no floating code holograms, no oversaturated purple lighting."*

### Archetype 3: The Impossible Object / High Visual Tension
A single striking, physical real-world object that makes the viewer stop and ask *"Wait, how does that work?"*
- **Example Concept:** *The Strawberry Problem / Tokenization*
- **Prompt:**
  > *"A striking macro studio photograph of a single fresh red strawberry cut precisely into three clean, separated geometric blocks on a matte slate surface. High-contrast directional studio lighting, crisp focus on the real fruit texture and natural seeds, soft natural drop shadow. Minimalist editorial style, documentary photography aesthetic. No glossy CG render, no glowing sci-fi lasers, no floating text."*

## 4. Standard Output Format (Copyable for Users)

Whenever generating thumbnail prompts, provide:
1. **The Direct ChatGPT Prompt** (in a clean copyable code block).
2. **The Visual Strategy** (explaining why this visual sparks curiosity).
3. **Recommended Thumbnail Text Overlay** (2–3 words max to place over the negative space).
